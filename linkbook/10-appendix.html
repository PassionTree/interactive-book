참고문헌 & 더 읽을거리

교과서

  • W. J. Dally, J. W. Poulton. Digital Systems Engineering. Cambridge University Press, 1998. — 신호, 전송선로, 타이밍, 동기화의 고전.
  • H. Johnson, M. Graham. High-Speed Digital Design: A Handbook of Black Magic. Prentice Hall, 1993. — 반사·종단·크로스토크의 실무 감각.
  • E. Bogatin. Signal and Power Integrity — Simplified, 3rd ed. Prentice Hall, 2018.
  • S. H. Hall, H. L. Heck. Advanced Signal Integrity for High-Speed Digital Designs. Wiley, 2009.
  • B. Razavi. Design of Integrated Circuits for Optical Communications, 2nd ed. Wiley, 2012. — CDR, 리미팅 증폭기, PLL.
